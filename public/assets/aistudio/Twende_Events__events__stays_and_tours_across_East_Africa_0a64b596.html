Rentals & apartments

Places to stay in and around Kampala, for a match weekend or a longer move. This is a guide, not a list of live availability: ask for current rooms and prices before you plan.

What you can rent

🏠 Short-stay apartments

Furnished flats for a few nights to a few weeks. Good for groups and families.

🏨 Guesthouses & lodges

Smaller, often family-run places with rooms and breakfast.

🛎️ Serviced apartments

Apartments with cleaning, security and sometimes meals. Usually priced by the night or week.

🔑 Long-term rentals

Monthly or yearly lets for people moving to Uganda for work or study.

🎓 Student & shared housing

Hostels and shared flats near campuses, usually the cheapest per person.

Areas visitors ask about

Kololo & Nakasero

Central and upmarket, close to restaurants, embassies and offices.

Bugolobi & Muyenga

Quiet residential areas with a mix of apartments and guesthouses.

Ntinda & Naguru

Residential areas with shops and cafés nearby.

Entebbe

Near the airport and Lake Victoria, handy for early or late flights.

Jinja

A few hours from Kampala, popular for river and adventure stays.

Area descriptions are general. Check distance, traffic and the route to the stadium before you book.

Before you pay

  • See the place first, or ask for a live video walk-through. Photos can be old or from another property.
  • Confirm who the landlord or agent is, and ask for the name and phone number on the booking.
  • Ask exactly what is included: water, power, internet, parking, security, cleaning.
  • Agree the price, dates, deposit and cancellation rules in writing, such as on WhatsApp or email.
  • Pay by a traceable method and ask for a receipt. Be careful about paying everything upfront to someone you haven't verified.
  • Never share your card number, PIN or passwords in chat.
  • For long-term lets, get a written tenancy agreement and read it before you sign.

Match-week tips

  • Book early. Rooms near the main host cities are likely to fill up around the opening day on 19 June 2027.
  • Ask about cancellation and date changes in case fixtures or travel plans move.
  • Check how you will get to the match and back, especially late at night.

Tours & travel

Ideas for what to do before or after the matches, and how to travel safely. Prices, permits and opening times change, so confirm them with the operator.

What to do

🏙️ Kampala & Entebbe

City tours, markets, museums, nightlife, the botanical gardens and the lakeshore.

🦁 Safaris & national parks

Game drives, boat trips and bird watching in Uganda's parks.

🦍 Gorilla & chimp trekking

Needs a permit booked in advance. Ask for permit rules and dates before you plan the rest of your trip.

🌊 Jinja & the Nile

River rafting, kayaking, quad biking and relaxed riverside stays.

🏝️ Lake Victoria islands

Boat trips and beach stays on islands such as the Ssese Islands.

🚌 Across the border

Add Kenya or Tanzania to your trip if you are following your team between host countries.

Getting around

  • Ride-hailing apps and licensed taxis are usually easier than flagging a vehicle on the street. Agree the fare before you start if there is no meter or app.
  • Boda bodas (motorbike taxis) are fast in traffic but riskier. Wear a helmet if you use one.
  • For long trips, hire a driver or join a group tour. Road travel in and out of cities can be slow on match days.
  • Keep your passport copy and booking details on your phone.

Planning checklist

  • Check current visa, health and entry requirements on official government sources before you travel.
  • Choose licensed tour operators and ask for a written itinerary, what is included and the cancellation terms.
  • Book national park permits and lodges well ahead of the tournament.
  • Keep a few days spare in your plan in case of delays.
  • Get travel insurance that covers medical care and cancellations.

This is general information, not a booking service. Always confirm details with the operator, park authority or official source.

About Twende Events

Twende means “let’s go” in Swahili. Twende Events is a community-led guide to what is happening and where to stay across East Africa. We bring together events, rentals and apartments, tours and travel, creatives and local partners in one place, so fans, visitors and locals can plan with confidence and businesses can be found.
